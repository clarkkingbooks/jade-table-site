Common questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the app, tap Customize, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Premium. Premium is a one-time purchase, so you never pay for it twice.

What does Premium include?

A one-time purchase that unlocks four animated premium tables and four premium card backs (available only with Premium), plus everything players otherwise earn by rank: every table, card back and soundtrack, and the 500 and 1,000 chips. It also removes ads, gives instant free chip restocks, and doubles your Daily Gift.

I ran out of chips. What now?

Claim your free Daily Gift from the gift button at the top of the table (it grows with your daily streak), or watch a short optional ad to restock. Restocks start at 1,000 chips and grow as you rank up. Premium members restock instantly, with no ad. You never have to pay for chips.

Can I win real money?

No. Jade Table Pai Gow uses play chips only. Chips have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold, or redeemed for money or prizes.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your chips, level, and achievements are saved on your device, so deleting the app erases them. A Premium purchase is never lost: you can restore it at any time.

How do I learn the rules?

Tap How Pai Gow works at the bottom of the table for the full rules and payouts, or switch to Training mode to practice setting hands with instant feedback.
