Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Jade Table Pai Gow app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by Clark King, a brand of Clark King Media LLC ("we," "us"). If you have questions, email info@clarkkingmedia.com.

The short version: you don't need an account, your game progress stays on your device, we never sell your information, and ads are optional to watch. Premium members see no ads at all.

Play chips only

The app is a simulated casino game for entertainment. It uses play chips only. Chips have no cash value and cannot be purchased, sold, or redeemed for money or prizes. The app is intended for adults 18 and older.

Information stored on your device

Your chip balance, game statistics, level, achievements, player name, chosen character, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information collected by our service providers

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you choose to watch an ad to restock chips or to double your Daily Gift, the ad is provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device type and settings, and how you interact with ads. On iPhone, the app asks your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking, and you can decline. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information in ads. Premium members are not shown ads.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Premium is purchased through Apple's App Store (or Google Play on Android), which handles all payment details. We never see your card or payment information. We use RevenueCat to confirm and restore your Premium purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, transaction details, and basic device information. Learn more in RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

How information is used

  • To show optional rewarded ads and give you chips for watching them
  • To unlock and restore Premium
  • To keep the app working and prevent fraud

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for any purpose not described here.

Your choices

  • Ad tracking: on iPhone, choose "Ask App Not to Track" when prompted, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads: watching ads is always optional. Premium removes them entirely.
  • Your data: to delete your on-device data, delete the app. For questions about information held by our service providers, or to make a privacy request, email us and we'll help.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
